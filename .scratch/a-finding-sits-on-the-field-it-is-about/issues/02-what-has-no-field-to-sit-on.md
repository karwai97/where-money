# 02 — What has no field to sit on

**What to build:** Someone who photographed something that is not a receipt is
told so across the whole form; the three remaining notices stop taking
permanent height and scroll with the fields.

`NotAReceipt` becomes a full-width banner above the form. It earns the room:
`Check.of` short-circuits and returns it alone, so nothing else is ever on
screen with it, and it is a claim about the whole photograph rather than about
a field to go and fix.

`ModelAskedForReview`, the refusal notice and the clean-Extraction line move
into the top of the scroll view. The Model's request for a human to look is a
hedge with frequently-empty reasons; the refusal is about the last commit
attempt; the clean line is one reassuring sentence. None is worth height on a
phone with the keyboard up, which is the complaint this feature answers.

No copy changes. Every key these four read stays as it is.
