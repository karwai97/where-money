# One Home Currency; foreign Expenses are stored but not aggregated

An Expense records the currency it was actually paid in. Only Home Currency
Expenses enter Rollups, charts and Recaps, and the count of what was excluded
is shown rather than hidden. Real FX — a rates provider, caching, historical
rates at purchase date, a rounding policy — is a subsystem that buys this
project nothing, and the prototype's habit of summing mixed currencies and
printing the total in red was a flagged gap rather than a design.
